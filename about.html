About

Clay Cup started with a simple idea: everyday coffee should feel personal. We wanted to bring handmade ceramics into daily life, without losing the care that goes into each piece.

So we partnered with three local potters in Lyon, who shape, glaze and fire every mug by hand.

Each mug is made slowly, in small batches of 15 pieces per potter, and each one is signed by its maker. Nothing is mass-produced. Every mug is a little different, and that is the point.

We are proud to work with local makers and to grow our community one cup at a time.
