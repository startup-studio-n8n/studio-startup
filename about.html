About

Clay Cup started with a simple idea: coffee tastes better in a cup made by hand. We wanted mugs that feel personal, objects you pick up every morning and recognise as your own.

Our mugs are made in Lyon by three local potters. Each potter makes about 20 pieces a month, so we produce around 60 mugs a month in total.

Every mug is shaped by hand, glazed and signed by the potter who made it. Because the work is done in small batches, each piece carries the care and the hands that made it.

Clay Cup. Handmade mugs for personal coffee moments, made in Lyon.
